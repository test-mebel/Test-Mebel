Product certification

Certification is carried out in accordance with Annex 2 to Resolution No. 502 and procedure PSK-02 “Product certification rules”. The applicant chooses the scheme from among those established for the product.

Attached to the application

  • an application stating the product name, HS code (CN FEA) and regulatory document;
  • a technical description of the item, set, suite or series of items (for scheme 1C);
  • for imported products — a copy of the shipping document bearing the mark of arrival in the customs territory of Uzbekistan (waybill, invoice, tax invoice);
  • information confirming rights to intellectual property objects (if any).

Stages

  1. review of the application and decision on it;
  2. product identification and sampling (PSK-09);
  3. testing in an accredited laboratory;
  4. production assessment (inspection) — for scheme 1C (PSK-11);
  5. review of results and decision on granting (refusing) the certificate;
  6. issuing the certificate in a unified electronic form and entering it in the state register;
  7. periodic assessment of certified serial products.

Certification of the management system is not required for schemes 1C, 3C, 4C; if a system certificate is available, it is taken into account in the application review.
