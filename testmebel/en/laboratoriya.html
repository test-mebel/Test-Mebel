Testing laboratories

Testing for certification is carried out only in accredited testing laboratories that have been evaluated by the body and included in its list of approved laboratories (PSK-08). A laboratory is selected during application review, provided the product and test methods fall within its accreditation scope; the applicant is informed of this in the decision on the application.

How laboratories are selected and monitored

  1. Accreditation status and scope are checked in the national register (akkred.uz).
  2. A written agreement including confidentiality and impartiality requirements is concluded.
  3. The body evaluates the laboratory and adds it to the list of approved laboratories.
  4. Every test report undergoes incoming review; the laboratory is monitored at least once a year.
  5. A laboratory whose accreditation is suspended or withdrawn is removed from the list.
