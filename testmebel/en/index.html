Online application

Which scheme suits you?

Applications are submitted through the certification information system. The applicant chooses the scheme; the body verifies its suitability for the product when reviewing the application.

1C

Serial production

Sample testing, production assessment (inspection) and periodic assessment of certified products. The first periodic assessment takes place no later than 12 months.

3C

Product batch

For a manufacturer or seller (importer). Identification, sampling, verification of intellectual property rights and testing.

4C

Single item

Certification by identifying a single item and testing it non-destructively.

Process

How certification works

  1. 01Application and reviewThe completeness of documents is checked and a decision on the application is made.
  2. 02Identification and samplingA specialist identifies the product on site and takes samples.
  3. 03TestingSamples are tested in an accredited testing laboratory (mainly our own).
  4. 04Evaluation and reviewResults are reviewed by a Technical Commission member who did not take part in the evaluation.
  5. 05Decision and certificateThe certificate is issued in a unified electronic form and entered in the register.
About us

Accuracy. Impartiality. Trust.

The certification body operates as a structural unit of “TEST MEBEL” LLC and is accredited for compliance with O‘z DSt ISO/IEC 17065:2015.

Every decision is based on sufficient evidence: the person who performed the evaluation does not make the decision on that application, and risks to impartiality are regularly analysed by the Impartiality Committee.

Frequently asked questions
Which products do you certify?
Furniture, wood-based panels and articles, plastic articles, paper and cardboard packaging, ceramic and glass tableware, metal household articles and paraffin candles. The full list is on the scope of accreditation page.
Which documents must accompany the application?
Product information, a technical description (for scheme 1C), a copy of the shipping document for imported products, and others. The list is on the Product certification page.
What if I disagree with the decision?
File an appeal. It is considered by the Appeals Commission within no more than one month. Details.
Does payment depend on the result?
No. Payment is made under the contract regardless of the certification result; staff salaries do not depend on the body’s revenue.
