Technical Commission

The Technical Commission consists of technical experts; it reviews evaluation results and makes decisions on granting (refusing to grant) certificates.

S.13

Review of results

Test reports and their incoming verification, identification and production assessment reports, and the applicant’s documents are reviewed.

S.14

Decision-making

The decision to grant or refuse a certificate is issued in the form given in Annex 7 to Resolution No. 502.

Independence rules

  • a member who took part in the evaluation for an application or advised the applicant does not take part in the review and decision for that application (O‘z DSt ISO/IEC 17065, 7.6);
  • before considering each application, a member confirms the absence of a conflict of interest;
  • a member affiliated with a testing laboratory does not consider applications in which that laboratory’s reports were used.
